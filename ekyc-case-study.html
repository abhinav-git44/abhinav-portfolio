From 4–5 days to instant: fixing eKYC drop-off

01The signal

Timbuckdo connects college students with part-time gigs. Looking at the analytics dashboard, the top of the funnel looked healthy: students were downloading the app, signing up, and coming back daily. But the number of job applications was very low compared with those numbers.

Active users who don't apply for jobs means the platform isn't delivering its core value — and employers don't get applicants. So the question was: where between "active" and "applied" are students getting stuck?

02Finding the leak

Breaking the journey into steps on the analytics dashboard showed one clear break. Students had to pass KYC verification before they could apply, and that is where they were dropping off.

Root cause

KYC was manual. After a student submitted documents, someone from the Timbuckdo team had to check them by hand, which took 4–5 days. During that wait, students couldn't apply for anything. Many lost interest or moved on, and never came back to apply.

Insight: this wasn't a motivation problem or a UI problem. Students wanted to apply — the process made them wait days for permission.

03Options I considered

There were two directions: make manual verification faster by adding people, or remove the manual step by automating it. For automation, DigiLocker allows instant, government-backed verification — and there were two ways to use it.

OptionTime to go liveCostVerification timeDecision
Add more people to manual verification — Recurring; grows with every new student Faster, but still manual Rejected — doesn't scale and is never instant
DigiLocker via Decentro's ready-made API Within a week ₹3–4 per verification Instant Chosen to launch immediately
Direct DigiLocker integration ~1 month to build One-time build effort; no per-verification fee Instant Built in parallel as the long-term solution

The trade-off

Direct integration was cheaper in the long run, but waiting a month meant another month of students dropping off. Decentro cost money per verification but could go live within a week. Instead of choosing one, I chose both, in sequence: buy speed now, build the cheaper option in the background, then switch.

Rollout

Phase 1

Launch with Decentro

Integrated Decentro's DigiLocker API so students could verify instantly within a week.

In parallel

Build direct DigiLocker integration

Asked the tech team to build the direct integration alongside (~1 month). Once it's built and working reliably, switch over and stop Decentro's pay-per-verification service.

Always on

Manual fallback

DigiLocker can fail — server errors and other issues happen. Students who can't complete it can still upload documents for manual review, so nobody is blocked.

05Results

  • KYC-stage drop-off fell from 42% to 18%.
  • Verified users grew 35% in one quarter.
  • Verification went from 4–5 days to instant for students using DigiLocker.
  • Manual review shrank to fallback cases only, instead of every student.

06What I learned

  • Healthy top-of-funnel numbers can hide a broken core action. Downloads and daily actives looked good; job applications told the real story.
  • Buy, then build. Paying per verification for a short time was cheaper than another month of lost students.
  • Always design the failure path. An automated flow with no fallback just moves the drop-off somewhere else.
